LUFFY 3D MODEL IMPORT GUIDE:
=============================
1. Generate or Download your 3D Luffy Model (.FBX or .OBJ):
   - Option A (Using your 4-View Reference Image):
     Upload your reference image to https://www.meshy.ai or https://www.tripo3d.ai and click Generate 3D.
     Download the result as .FBX format.
   - Option B (Free Pre-Made Model):
     Search "Luffy One Piece FBX" on Sketchfab.com or Models-resource.com and download for free.

2. Place the .fbx file into this folder:
   Assets/_Project/Art/Characters/ (or Assets/Resources/ as LuffyModel)

3. In Unity:
   - Select the 'Player' GameObject in the Hierarchy.
   - In the Inspector, locate 'PlayerCharacterVisuals' component.
   - Drag and drop your Luffy 3D model into the 'Custom Model Prefab' slot.
   - Press Play!
